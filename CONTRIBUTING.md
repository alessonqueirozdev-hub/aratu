# Contributing to Aratu

Thank you for wanting to help. Before a contribution can be merged:

- The repository is written in English: code, comments, commit messages and
  documentation. The course and the manual are kept in Portuguese, Spanish
  and English, and a change to one goes to the three.
- Every new piece of syntax comes with a section of the course, an example
  and an exercise, in the three languages, and an entry in
  `crates/aratu/tests/book.rs`.
- Dependencies are MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC or Zlib only. No code is
  copied from projects under the GPL, LGPL, AGPL or MPL, nor from JUCE.
- `sh scripts/check.sh` passes.
