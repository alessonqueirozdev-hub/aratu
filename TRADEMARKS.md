name "Aratu", its logo and the Aratu crab mascot identify the project and
its author, Alesson Lucas Oliveira de Queiroz, and the licence does not
grant them (section 7(e) of the GNU AGPL).

Welcome, without asking:

- saying that a piece, a class, a talk or a project is made with Aratu, or
  that a program is based on Aratu;
- writing about Aratu, teaching it, and showing the mascot when doing so;
- a modified version that says it is based on Aratu, under a name of its
  own.

Not without written permission:

- calling a modified version, a product or a service "Aratu", or a name
  that could be taken for it;
- using the mascot or the logo as the identity of something else;
- suggesting that the author made, endorses or supports what he did not.
