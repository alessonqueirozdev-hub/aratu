Aratu is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License, version
3 or later, with the additional terms in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). Anyone may
use, study, change and share it under those terms, including in a business.

The AGPL asks for something in return: whoever conveys Aratu, or a version
of it, or lets people use it over a network, must offer them the complete
source code of that work under the same licence, and keep the attribution
to its author.

When that does not suit, Aratu can be licensed commercially instead. A
commercial licence lets you, for example:

- build Aratu, or a version of it, into a product or a service whose own
  code stays closed;
- run a modified Aratu as a service without publishing its source;
- ship Aratu inside an app, an instrument or a device under your own terms.

Teaching, research, personal projects and performances need no commercial
licence: the AGPL covers them, as long as its terms are kept.
